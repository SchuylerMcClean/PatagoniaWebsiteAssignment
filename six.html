Kayaking

Kayaking in Patagonia is an epic way to explore some of the wildest and most untouched parts of South America—think massive glaciers, fjords, turquoise rivers, and remote coastal inlets. Here’s what you need to know before paddling out:

Best Time of Year?

Peak Season: November - March (summer in southern hemisphere)

Gear & Preparation

Clothing: Dry suit or waterproof layers
Gear: Spray skirt, paddles, dry bags for clothes and gear
Navigation: Map, GPS, Offline maps

Wildlife & Scenery

Common Sightings: Sea lions, dolphins, penguins, condors, and whales
Scenery: Glaciers, snowcapped peaks, fjords, and forests

Access Points

Chile: Puerto Natales (Torres del Paine), Puerto Río Tranquilo (Lake General Carrera, Marble Caves), Punta Arenas (Tierra del Fuego, Beagle Channel)
Argentina: Bariloche (Lakes District), El Calafate (for glacial lakes, though kayaking is more limited here)

Flying In & Travel Logistics

Chile: Fly into Punta Arenas or Balmaceda
Argentina: Fly into San Carlos de Bariloche
