Camping

Camping in Patagonia is one of the best ways to fully immerse yourself in its raw, wild beauty. Whether you're pitching a tent under jagged peaks or lakeside in the quiet of the forest, here's everything you need to know:

Best Time of Year?

Peak Season: November - March (summer in southern hemisphere)

Types of Camping

Designated Campgrounds: Tent platforms, toilets, cooking shelters, etc.
Backcountry camping: Allowed in some areas, restrictions may apply

Top Places to Camp

Torres del Paine National Park: Permits needed in advance
Los Glaciers National Park: Permits and reservations typically not required

Gear & Preparation

Typical camping gear, especially warm layers for drastic weather

Camping in Patagonia is about more than just sleeping outside—it’s about being part of the landscape. If you're up for the challenge, it’s one of the most rewarding ways to experience this legendary region.
