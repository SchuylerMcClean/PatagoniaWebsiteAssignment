  • Bariloche (Argentina) – Home to Cerro Catedral, the largest ski resort in South America
  • Las Leñas (Argentina) – Steep terrain, deep powder, and great off-piste skiing
  • Chapelco (Argentina) – Family-friendly resort with scenic views of Lanín Volcano

  • Antillanca (Chile) – Great backcountry skiing on volcanic terrain

What to know:

Best Time of Year?

Winter Season: June - September
Spring Season: October - November (backcountry & volcano skiing still possible)

Gear & Preparation

Resort Skiing: Standard ski gear, but bring extra layers for wind protection
Backcountry Skiing: Avalanche gear (beacon, probe, shovel), skins, and GPS/navigation tools are essential

Safety & Avalanche Protection

Avalanche Awareness: Backcountry skiing requires proper training—consider hiring a local guide
Remote Areas: Some ski zones have limited rescue services, so be self-sufficient

Flying In & Travel Logistics

Argentina: Fly into Bariloche (BRC) for Cerro Catedral or Mendoza (MDZ) for Las Leñas
Chile: Fly into Temuco (ZCO) for volcano skiing or Santiago (SCL) for Portillo
