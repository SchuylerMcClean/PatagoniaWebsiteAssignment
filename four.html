Rockclimbing

What to know:

Best Time of Year?

Peak Season: November - March (best weather)
Shoulder Season: October and April (fewer crowds)

Popular Climbing Destinations

Fitz Roy & Cerro Torre (Argentina, Los Glaciers region)
Torres del Paine & Cerro Castillo (Chile)

Permits and Reservations

Fitz Roy & Cerro Torre: Climbing registration required
Torres del Paine: Some routes require permits

Weather

Often unpredictable, sudden weather changes may be frequent

Gear and Preparation

Climbing gear: Full alpine rack, ice tooks, typical climbing gear
Clothing: Waterproof and windproof layers
Navigation: Map, GPS, Offline maps, Satellite devices

Flying In

Torres del Paine: Fly into Punta Arenas, bus to Puerto Natales
Fitz Roy & Cerro Torre: Fly into El Calafate, bus to El Chalten
