Hiking

Patagonia is a trekker’s paradise, offering some of the most stunning and iconic trails in the world. Whether you're tackling a multi-day adventure or a scenic day hike, Patagonia’s treks promise unforgettable landscapes and a true connection with nature.

The W Trek and O Circuit in Torres del Paine (Chile) take hikers through breathtaking valleys, past turquoise lakes, and up close to the park’s legendary granite spires. In Argentina, Los Glaciares National Park features the epic Laguna de los Tres hike, leading to awe-inspiring views of Mount Fitz Roy, while the Laguna Torre trail offers a dramatic perspective of Cerro Torre.

What to know:

Best Time of Year?

Peak Season: November - March (best weather)
Shoulder Season: October and April (fewer crowds)

Popular Destinations

Torres del Paine (O circuit, W trek)
Los Glaciers (Laguna de los Tres, Laguna Torre)

Permits and Reservations

Torres del Paine: O circuit requires permit, campgrounds require reservation
Los Glaciers: no permits for day hikes, campgrounds may require reservation

Weather

Often unpredictable, sudden weather changes may be frequent

Important Items to Pack

Clothing: Waterproof and windproof layers, hiking boots etc.
Navigation: Map, GPS, Offline maps (limited cell service)

Flying In

Chile: Fly into Punta Arenas, bus to Puerto Natales, Torres del Paine
Argentina: Fly into El Calafate, bus to El Chalten, Los Glaciers
